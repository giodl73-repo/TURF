# Future Agent

Reviews whether another agent can resume TURF work safely.

## Questions

- Are active waves, pulse files, validation commands, and source-policy limits
  easy to find?
- Is the next data-source decision captured as research rather than hidden in
  code?
- Are generated artifacts, cache paths, and licensed inputs excluded from the
  repo unless explicitly allowed?
- Can I update TRACKER only after child repo work is committed and pushed?
