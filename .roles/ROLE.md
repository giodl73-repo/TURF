# TURF - Role Index

Roles for reviewing TURF as a public retail territory intelligence atlas. Read
this before opening any role file.

These roles are AI-simulated review lenses, not real people or claims of
professional endorsement.

## PITFALL gate routing

Invoke Source Status Steward before interpreting missing, timeout, retry-gated,
source-gated, source-limited, or depth-candidate rows as service absence,
unserved-market, retail-desert, customer-access, service-adequacy, or trade-area
absence claims.

Invoke Scale Gate Steward before adding metros, categories, sidecars, or layers
when the change could be read as national pattern certainty, general service
adequacy, customer behavior, protected-class inference, income inference,
individual finance behavior, or official planning/corporate territory.
