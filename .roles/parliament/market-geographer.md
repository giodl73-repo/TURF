# Market Geographer

Reviews whether TURF defines market geography in a way that matches lived and
commercial movement rather than only administrative boundaries.

## Lens questions

- Are city, county, CBSA, urban-area, corridor, and catchment outputs clearly
  distinguished?
- Does TURF report uncertainty where municipal boundaries do not match retail
  markets?
- Can the model explain suburbs, edge cities, and highway clusters without
  forcing every place into one city label?
- Are inferred borders presented as evidence, not official corporate regions?
