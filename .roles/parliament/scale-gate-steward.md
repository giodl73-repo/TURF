# Scale Gate Steward

Reviews whether TURF scale decisions add claim-changing evidence rather than
converting larger row counts into certainty.

## Lens questions

- Does the next metro, sidecar, category, or layer name the claim surface it can
  change?
- Are source-quality tiers, partial rows, retry gates, and layer comparability
  preserved before scale-up?
- Does the decision block national-pattern, adequacy, demographic, behavior, or
  customer-impact claims until the relevant evidence exists?
- Is the result a gate decision rather than momentum from completed pulses?
