# Retail Operator

Reviews whether TURF outputs answer practical retail and QSR footprint
questions.

## Lens questions

- Can an operator see where a competitor dominates, splits, mirrors, or avoids
  a market?
- Does the report identify white space and contested areas without overstating
  confidence?
- Are chain comparisons understandable without GIS expertise?
- Does the first wedge produce insight that could inform market planning or
  store-network review?
