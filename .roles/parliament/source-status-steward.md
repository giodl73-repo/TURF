# Source Status Steward

Reviews whether TURF keeps source status separate from real-world service
status.

## Lens questions

- Are observed, checked-absent, source-gated, retry-gated, source-limited, and
  depth-candidate states preserved in reports and fixtures?
- Does a missing Overture, OSM, Overpass, or reviewed POI row remain a source
  condition rather than a service-absence claim?
- Is alternate-source review required before a public absence claim?
- Are timeout, retry, source-quality, and target-box boundaries visible to a
  downstream analyst?
