# Source Policy Auditor

Reviews whether TURF keeps source rights, cache policy, and terms boundaries
clear.

## Lens questions

- Are public, user-supplied, commercial, and validation-only sources labeled?
- Does the repo avoid storing raw proprietary POI exports or scraped locator
  responses?
- Are official store directories used only in ways their terms allow?
- Do reports cite source family, vintage, and known coverage limits?
