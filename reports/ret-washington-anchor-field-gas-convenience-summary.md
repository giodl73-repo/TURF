## Learning

Gas/convenience adds a car-trip and quick-errand signal that behaves
differently from finance. Bellevue core is finance-dense but relatively
gas/convenience-light inside the tight downtown field. South Hill / Puyallup,
Southcenter / Tukwila, Kitsap Mall / Silverdale, and Tacoma Mall show stronger
car-trip service texture.

That helps separate downtown multi-errand fields from auto-oriented mall and
suburban service fields before TURF scales the same context contract to more
urban areas.
