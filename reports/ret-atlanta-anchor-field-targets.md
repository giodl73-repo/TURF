## Goal

The reasonable next goal is to prove whether the completed Washington
11-dimension context contract travels to Atlanta before TURF scales to more
urban areas.

- gas / convenience
- gym
- hardware
- laundromat

## Learning Hypotheses

Post offices and libraries should tell us whether these retail fields align with
civic service geography or only with commercial capacity.

Parks and transit should separate walkable urban fields from car-trip fields.

Banks, pharmacies, gas/convenience, gyms, hardware, dollar stores, and
laundromats should tell us whether a field behaves like a finance/wellness
district, household-service district, auto/service corridor, value-errand node,
or broad everyday grid.

## Before More Metros

Do not scale to a third urban area until the Atlanta target set has:

- one checked output per active context dimension
- zero source-gated dimensions in the Atlanta context summary
- a Washington-vs-Atlanta archetype comparison
- a short note on which field types recur and which are Atlanta-specific
