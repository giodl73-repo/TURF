# A.1b Minimum Postal Parish

## Thesis

The smallest modern TURF enclave may be a minimum postal parish: a place made
legible by postal/address infrastructure and only a very small everyday-service
stack.

The working intuition is:

- post office only,
- post office plus QSR,
- post office plus gas/convenience,
- post office plus dollar store,
- post office plus grocery/pharmacy,
- postal locality with no observed national-chain sidecar in the current
  evidence stack.

This is a research extension of `A.1+postal-parish`. It is not a national claim
yet. TURF's current 43-field profile is mostly selected urban and metro anchor
fields, so it cannot answer "smallest postal parishes in the country" without
a new national or rural/postal-place evidence pass.
