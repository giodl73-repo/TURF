## Question

The Mississippi current-anchor gate kept five of seven local postal-only
candidates in the live-current-anchor queue. This depth review asks whether
alternate open sources immediately contradict those five target-box
classifications.

## Evidence

Alternate open sources reject three candidates as postal-only under the sampled
target-box rule:

- Tutwiler has Big Papa's inside the box and a state food-facility listing for
  Double Quick #49 in the same postal place.
- Phillip/Philipp has Phillip Grocery inside the sampled box.
- Crowder has Dollar General inside the sampled box.

Union Church survives only with a nearby-service constraint. Pleasant Hill
Grocery is Union Church-addressed, but Census geocodes it just east of the
sampled box.

Valley Park survives as a strict target-box candidate after the source gate is
resolved. Fast Lane - No 119 shares its phone/address with MSDH's Circle K
#2723446 listing in Vicksburg, and Census geocodes that address outside the
sampled Valley Park target box.

## Decision

The Mississippi depth review narrows the five current-anchor-confirmed
postal-only queue:

- rejected as postal-only: Tutwiler, Phillip/Philipp, Crowder,
- survives with nearby-service constraint: Union Church,
- strict target-box survivor with outside-box service constraint: Valley Park.

Bigbee Valley and Nitta Yuma were already removed from current-anchor numerator
consideration by the current-anchor gate.

## Boundary

This review may reject or weaken sampled target-box candidates. It may not
claim Mississippi frequency, Deep South Delta frequency, national frequency,
service adequacy, trade areas, catchments, income, spending, customer behavior,
or complete local-commerce coverage.
