## Question

The Wyoming/Mountain West local Geofabrik sidecar pass classified two of twelve
target boxes as `postal_only_candidate`: Auburn and Carpenter WY. Both passed
the current-postal-anchor gate. This depth review asks whether alternate open
sources immediately contradict strict postal-only target-box status.

## Evidence

Auburn is no longer cleanly postal-only. USPS confirms the Auburn post office
inside the sampled target box, and Star Valley Sod also geocodes inside the box.
That source is a local landscape/sod trade-service and retail pickup signal,
not food service or consumer gas/convenience. Nearby restaurant evidence points
toward Afton several miles away and remains outside the sampled box.

Carpenter remains the strict target-box survivor in this review. USPS confirms
the Carpenter post office inside the sampled target box. Antelope Truck Stop
and TA Cheyenne are real fuel, food, and laundry/service sources in the broader
Carpenter/Burns context, but both geocode outside the Carpenter target box.

## Decision

The Wyoming depth review narrows the postal-only queue:

- rejected for local trade-service signal, but not food or gas/convenience:
  Auburn,
- strict target-box survivor: Carpenter WY.

This turns the Mountain West pass from "two current-anchor-confirmed
postal-only candidates" into "one strict survivor and one trade-service
taxonomy refinement."
