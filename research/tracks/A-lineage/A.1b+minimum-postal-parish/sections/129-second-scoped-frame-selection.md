## Decision

Select **Alaska / Arctic Remote** as the second scoped full-frame replication
target.

This is the next depth step after Wyoming because Wyoming now has a reviewed
internal scoped target-box claim packet. Alaska was predeclared as the second
choice and tests a different failure mode: remote service systems, fragile
street-address geocoding, and thinner open-source coverage.

## Why Alaska

Alaska has a tractable complete seed frame for a second implementation:

- 108 public post-office seed rows,
- a prior 12-row selected-wave sample,
- four first-pass postal-only candidates in that sample,
- current-anchor and depth-review behavior already exercised,
- one constrained selected-wave survivor at Clam Gulch.

The existing Alaska selected-wave artifacts are useful inputs, but they are not
the scoped frame. The next implementation must promote Alaska from a 12-row
selected sample to all 108 public seed rows before any scoped Alaska share can
exist.

## Deferred Candidate

Mississippi / Deep South Delta remains the third scoped candidate. It is useful
for recurrence and outside-box service repair behavior, but it has a larger
184-row public seed frame and should follow Alaska unless the goal changes.

## Boundary

This section only selects the next scoped frame. It does not create an Alaska
denominator, numerator, estimator, frequency claim, complete-service-absence
claim, catchment claim, or service-adequacy claim.
