## Contract

The Alaska scoped frame starts from the existing Arctic Remote public
post-office seed artifact and must promote it from selected-wave sampling to a
complete scoped frame.

Required outputs:

- seed exclusion ledger,
- full scoped target boxes for the 108 public post-office seed rows unless
  explicit exclusions change the count,
- enriched target rows with settlement proxy joins,
- food service, gas/convenience, dollar store, hardware, and laundromat sidecar
  reviews and status files,
- minimum-stack classification for every scoped target,
- current-anchor review for first-pass postal-only candidates,
- source-gated treatment where current anchors cannot be confirmed,
- depth review for current-anchor-confirmed candidates,
- denominator eligibility rows and exclusion ledger,
- estimator dry run,
- method review and claim packet only after all upstream gates reconcile.

## Gates

No denominator row may be clean unless its seed semantics, settlement join,
minimum-stack classification, current-anchor/depth status, and exclusion status
are reconciled.

No numerator row may advance without a current inside-box postal anchor and
completed alternate-source everyday-service depth review.

No scoped Alaska share may be reported until denominator eligibility rows plus
the exclusion ledger reconcile to the scoped target count.

## Boundary

The allowed eventual output is only an exact scoped Alaska / Arctic Remote
target-box dry-run count after full-frame review. This contract blocks Arctic
regional frequency, national frequency, catchment isolation, complete service
absence, service adequacy, and ZIP/city/county/trade-area claims.
