## Direction

The next A.1b phase should go breadth first, then depth. The current pilots
already show that post office alone is too thin as a minimum-stack claim. The
larger unknown is whether that pattern holds across different settlement
systems.

## Breadth First

The breadth pass should start with diverse regional strata:

- New England,
- Appalachia,
- Great Plains,
- Mountain West,
- Southwest Borderlands,
- California Interior,
- Island and Remote communities,
- existing Pacific Northwest, Deep South, and Great Lakes/Midwest baselines.

The goal is not to find one more interesting example. The goal is to stress the
working claim across settlement systems.

## Then Depth

Depth should follow only where breadth produces outliers:

- possible postal-only candidates,
- food-service-only minimum stacks,
- high source-gated regions,
- island, remote, tribal, resort, border, or county-seat edge cases,
- places where OSM semantics blur post office, private shipping, or local
  service evidence.

## Launch Gate

The breadth phase starts when these exist:

1. a reviewed open post-office seed extract for at least one new stratum,
2. a versioned open settlement-size join for that extract,
3. a passing preflight report,
4. a filled manifest with real artifact paths.

## Boundary

This roadmap may define the next research sequence. It may not claim national
frequency, complete coverage, smallest postal parish, service adequacy, trade
areas, catchments, income, spending, or customer behavior.
