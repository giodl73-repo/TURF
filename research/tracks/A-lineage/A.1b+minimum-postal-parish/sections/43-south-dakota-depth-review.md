## Question

The South Dakota local Geofabrik sidecar pass classified seven of twelve target
boxes as `postal_only_candidate`. This depth review asks whether alternate open
sources immediately contradict those target-box classifications.

## Evidence

Alternate open sources reject four candidates as postal-only under the sampled
target-box rule:

- Alcester has Total Stop Food Store inside the box, with food, grocery, gas,
  and quick-eat evidence.
- Alexandria has Friendly's/Sinclair inside the box, with fuel,
  convenience-store, hot-deli, and Pizza Hut Express evidence.
- Allen has Pass Creek Market, formerly Allen Store, inside the box, with
  grocery and fuel evidence.
- Astoria has Astoria Village Inn/Village Inn inside the box as food-service
  evidence.
- Avon has Ron's Market and Tolsma's Cove inside the box as grocery and food
  evidence.

Badger is also no longer cleanly postal-only: Badger Oil geocodes inside the
target box and is listed as storefront retail, automotive repair, bulk
petroleum, and mower sales. This is not food-service or consumer
gas/convenience confirmation, but it is a local everyday/trade-service signal.

Artesian is the only strict target-box survivor in this review. The
Sinclair/Country Pumper source is real and has fuel, convenience-store, and
restaurant/QSR evidence, but it geocodes west of the sampled target box. It is
nearby Artesian-addressed service, not an inside-box contradiction.

## Decision

The South Dakota depth review materially narrows the postal-only queue:

- rejected as postal-only: Alcester, Alexandria, Allen, Astoria, Avon,
- rejected for local trade-service signal, but not food-service: Badger,
- strict target-box survivor: Artesian.

This turns the first Great Plains pass from "seven possible postal-only
targets" into "one strict survivor and six alternate-source rejections or
taxonomy refinements."

## Boundary

This review may reject or weaken sampled target-box candidates. It may not
claim South Dakota frequency, Great Plains frequency, national frequency,
service adequacy, trade areas, catchments, income, spending, customer behavior,
or complete local-commerce coverage.
