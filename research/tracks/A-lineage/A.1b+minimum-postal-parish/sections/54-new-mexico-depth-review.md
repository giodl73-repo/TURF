## Question

The New Mexico/Southwest Borderlands local Geofabrik sidecar pass classified two
of twelve target boxes as `postal_only_candidate`: Algodones and Amistad NM.
Both passed the current-postal-anchor gate. This depth review asks whether
alternate open sources immediately contradict strict postal-only target-box
status.

## Evidence

Algodones is no longer cleanly postal-only. The current post-office anchor
geocodes inside the sampled target box, but Algodones Market also geocodes
inside the same box as grocery retail. Family Dollar provides additional
community retail context, but its intersection address remains geocode-gated.
Black Mesa Travel Center and the Pueblo Restaurant are real food,
convenience-store, truck-stop, and laundromat context, but 26 Hagen Rd geocodes
north of the sampled target box and is therefore outside-box evidence only.

Amistad remains the strict target-box survivor in this review. USPS confirms
the Amistad post office inside the sampled target box. The reviewed
inside-box fire-department evidence is civic evidence, not everyday-service
evidence. McDaniel Water and Amistad Land and Cattle are local service or trade
signals in the broader Amistad context, but both geocode outside the sampled
target box.

## Decision

The New Mexico depth review narrows the postal-only queue:

- rejected for inside-box everyday retail service: Algodones,
- strict target-box survivor: Amistad NM.

This turns the Southwest Borderlands pass from "two current-anchor-confirmed
postal-only candidates" into "one strict survivor and one everyday-service
reclassification."
