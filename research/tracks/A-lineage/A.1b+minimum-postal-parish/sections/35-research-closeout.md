## Current Finding

The current A.1b phase is complete as a bounded pilot and method scaffold.
Across Washington, Georgia, and Illinois, the research found no post-office-only
minimum postal parish among the classified post-office-observed targets.

The working finding is narrower and stronger:

> In the bounded three-state pilot, the observed minimum postal stack is post
> office plus named food service, not post office alone.

## Evidence Base

The pilot evidence now includes:

- 60 target boxes across Washington, Georgia, and Illinois,
- 28 post-office-observed classified target boxes,
- zero postal-only candidates among classified post-office-observed targets,
- named food-service evidence in all 28 classified post-office-observed target
  boxes,
- completed source-gate, builder, validator, enrichment, preflight, and
  manifest scaffolding for the next sampled pass.

## What This Phase Can Claim

This phase can claim:

- the original post-office-only hypothesis failed in the three-state pilot,
- broad food-service evidence is required before calling a place postal-only,
- narrow national-chain QSR layers are insufficient for minimum-stack work,
- the next research move should be breadth-first sampling, not more ad hoc
  depth in the same pilot set.

## What This Phase Cannot Claim

This phase cannot claim:

- national frequency,
- the smallest postal parish in the United States,
- complete post-office inventory,
- complete local-commerce inventory,
- service adequacy,
- trade areas or catchments,
- income, spending, or customer behavior.
