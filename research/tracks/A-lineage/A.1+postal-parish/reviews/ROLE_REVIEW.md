# Role Review: A.1 Postal Parish Scaffold

These are AI-simulated review lenses, not real people or professional
endorsements.

## Market Geographer

Status: pass with boundary constraint.

The scaffold separates postal addressability from municipality, Census place,
county, CBSA, ZCTA, retail field, and historical analogy. Future drafting must
keep ZIP Codes distinct from Census ZCTAs and avoid polygon language unless the
geometry source is explicit.

## Source Policy Auditor

Status: pass.

The module points to repo-generated reports, checked CSVs, public Census/OSM
evidence, and future citations. It blocks restricted USPS address files,
unsupported retailer-locator reuse, proprietary POI data, and demographic or
customer-behavior claims.

## Catchment Modeler

Status: pass.

The module treats postal evidence as addressability and civic-service context,
not a nearest-store, drive-time, gravity, or franchise-territory assignment.

## Atlas Designer

Status: pass.

The lineage table can become a map legend or dashboard filter because it maps
historical function to modern institution, TURF evidence, example fields, and
claim boundary.

## Market Analyst

Status: pass.

The scaffold links exact reports, CSVs, queries, and fixtures. That keeps the
module tied to TURF evidence rather than detached historical commentary.

## Future Agent
