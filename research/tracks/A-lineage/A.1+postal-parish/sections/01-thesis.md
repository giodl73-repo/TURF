# Thesis

Postal and address infrastructure is TURF's first lineage module because it
provides the practical national layer that makes places addressable before a
retail footprint is analyzed.

Working thesis:

> In the United States, postal addressing, ZIPs, post offices, delivery
> localities, and Census ZCTA approximations often act as a modern
> parish-style addressability layer: they organize daily life around reachable
> service points and named local geographies even when municipal, Census,
> county, or lived-neighborhood boundaries do not line up.

This module studies that function as an analogy. It does not treat postal
geography as a legal parish, a church institution, a cultural identity, an
official corporate territory, or a customer-behavior claim.

## Research Questions

- Which TURF fields carry direct post-office signal?
- Which fields lack direct post-office signal but remain strongly typed by
  grocery, pharmacy, mass retail, retail-complex, QSR, auto-service, finance,
  or gas/convenience evidence?
- Where does postal civic evidence reinforce a field family, and where does it
  merely provide addressability context?
- How should TURF describe postal geography without confusing ZIP Codes, ZCTAs,
  municipalities, Census places, counties, CBSAs, and retail catchments?
