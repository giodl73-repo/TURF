## Layer Read

Postal legibility is not scored in this packet. A.1/A.1b remains the baseline
method for addressability and civic-presence claims, but CEP-006 is a
mall/provisioning and light service-quarter control packet.

A.2 supplies mall/provisioning context. Silverdale is treated as a West Sound
dense-grid / mall-service control. Existing auto-parts fixture evidence places
auto-service rows in Silverdale, while the Silverdale pizza sidecar timed out
after retry. That pizza result is source-gated, not checked absent.

A.3 supplies only partial context. Kitsap / Silverdale appears as surrounding
anchor context for ferry-side nodes such as Kingston and Bainbridge. CEP-006
does not have direct ferry-terminal or movement-sidecar packet rows, so its
movement layer remains partial/contextual.

A.4 supplies the light service-quarter role. The service-quarter depth synthesis
classifies Kitsap Mall / Silverdale as
`base_service_quarter_with_real_estate_only_extension`: finance, tax prep,
insurance, and real-estate layers are observed; LAN and loan/mortgage profiles
are checked absent only.

## Packet Claim

Kitsap Mall / Silverdale can be used as a current-enclave light-control case
where mall/provisioning context and light service-quarter texture are present,
but direct movement evidence is partial and LAN / loan-mortgage gaps are
bounded-profile results only.

The packet should not be used as a full professional-service texture, ferry
movement-node claim, official boundary, trade-area claim, delivery-area claim,
spending estimate, demand model, legal/accounting/notary absence claim, lender
absence claim, service-adequacy measure, or individual-behavior inference.
