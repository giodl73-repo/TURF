# Ferry-Side Full-Stack Closeout

This file completes Phase 2 work item FS-005 for the ferry-side full-stack
comparison lane.

## Closeout Decision

Promote CEP-005 Kingston WA from design-only to caveated scored ferry-side
packet.

Do not promote it to a clean full-stack packet.

The new packet status is:

`packet_ready_with_caveat`

The new packet role is:

`ferry_side_town_provisioning_with_service_quarter_caveats`

## Why Promotion Is Supported

Kingston is no longer merely a design example. Phase 2 adds:

- A.4 service-quarter scoring with profile caveats,
- bounded provisioning breadth beyond Domino's,
- a restated ferry movement layer grounded in route and terminal-cluster
  evidence,
- selected controls: Kitsap Mall / Silverdale and Bremerton WA.

That is enough to score Kingston across the central spine: town/provisioning,
ferry movement, and service-quarter texture.

## Why Clean Full-Stack Is Not Supported

The service-quarter layer is not as clean as the stronger full-stack controls.
Insurance, real-estate, and loan/mortgage texture include locality or
office-footprint caveats. Lodging is peripheral rather than immediate
ferry-core evidence. Bremerton remains the stronger ferry/town control because
it has both direct ferry-terminal evidence and dense multi-brand pizza texture.

## Comparison Result

Against Kitsap Mall / Silverdale, Kingston is stronger on ferry movement and
weaker/caveated on service-quarter texture.

Against Bremerton, Kingston is thinner on town/provisioning breadth and weaker
as a ferry/town control, but it is now scoreable rather than design-only.
