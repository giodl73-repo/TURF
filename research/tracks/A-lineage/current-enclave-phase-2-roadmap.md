# Current Enclave Phase 2 Roadmap

Phase 2 should move by depth before breadth.

The first atlas is publication-ready for the selected stack-typology claim. The
next work should not add more places merely to widen coverage. It should test
one deferred claim surface deeply enough to decide whether the atlas needs a
new packet role or whether the current typology already handles the case.

## Roadmap Decision

Select ferry-side full-stack comparison as the next depth lane.

Kingston is the right next field because it sits on the central spine rather
than on a post-office-only branch:

- A.1/A.1b: addressability and civic-presence baseline remains available but
  should not control the case.
- A.2: Domino's/pizza and thin provisioning show why sparse four-brand QSR
  evidence is not food-service absence.
- A.3: WSDOT route context and ferry-terminal retry evidence make the field a
  movement node.
- A.4: service-quarter texture is deliberately unscored in CEP-005 and is the
  missing layer needed for a full-stack comparison.

This makes Kingston a better next phase than a 50-state post-office push. The
question is not "does every place have a post office?" The question is how
older enclave functions reappear as current addressability, provisioning,
movement, and service-quarter stacks.

## Lane Ranking

1. Ferry-side full-stack comparison: chosen now. It can convert a design-only
   contrast into a scored comparison without changing the publication-ready
   atlas claim.
2. Lender-edge texture: useful after ferry-side work if service-quarter gaps
   become the main story.
3. Truck-stop/freight-service coverage: useful only if the claim shifts from
   corridor-service texture to truck/freight completeness.
4. Kitsap Mall / Silverdale full-stack upgrade: useful as a control upgrade
   after the ferry-side lane defines the comparison need.
5. Broad post-office, national category, consulting, and generic
   business-service breadth: deferred. These add volume but do not currently
   improve the central-spine claim.

## Phase 2 Deliverables

- `current-enclave-ferry-side-full-stack-launch.md`
- `current-enclave-ferry-side-full-stack-workplan-2026-08-16.csv`
- A future Kingston A.4 service-quarter scoring packet, if the launch gate is
  accepted.
- A future ferry-side comparison closeout that decides whether CEP-005 remains
  design-only or becomes a scored full-stack packet.

## Claim Boundary

Phase 2 does not claim ferry catchments, route choice, wait time, demand,
delivery territory, service adequacy, food access, resident identity, income,
credit, wealth, professional quality, or direct historical descent.

It only asks whether ferry-side current-enclave fields can be scored across the
same central-spine layers used by the first atlas.
