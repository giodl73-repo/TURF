## Core Retail Layers

The first evidence read should compare retail-complex, grocery, mass-retail,
and pharmacy summary reports across Chicago, Dallas/Fort Worth, Los Angeles /
Inland Empire, and Philadelphia / South Jersey.

These layers let A.2 distinguish:

- mall-led regional anchors,
- edge-city big-box power fields,
- grocery/pharmacy everyday grids,
- legacy mall service fields,
- specialized town-center fields.

## Boundary

This module should preserve the same discipline as A.1: observed footprint,
bounded field, historical analogy, source gate, checked absence, and future
model hypothesis are separate evidence classes.
