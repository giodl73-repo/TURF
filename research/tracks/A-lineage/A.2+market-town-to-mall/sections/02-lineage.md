# Lineage

Market towns and market centers historically concentrated exchange,
provisioning, services, routes, records, and periodic gathering. A.2 does not
try to prove direct descent from those older forms. It asks whether modern
retail anchor fields perform comparable functions in public footprint evidence.

## Function

The market-center layer helps answer:

- Where does repeated retail/service activity cluster?
- Which field is organized by a mall, retail complex, grocery/pharmacy grid, or
  big-box anchor?
- Which fields serve local everyday errands versus regional comparison
  shopping?
- Which fields behave like legacy centers whose anchor function is still
  visible but no longer dominant?

## Modern Translation

The modern institution is a stack:

- malls and retail complexes,
- grocery and pharmacy grids,
- mass retail and warehouse-club anchors,
- big-box power centers,
- surrounding everyday-service and sidecar layers.

TURF treats this stack as commercial-footprint evidence. It does not turn it
into an official town, municipal center, neighborhood, or corporate territory.
