# Thesis

Working thesis:

> Retail complexes, malls, grocery/pharmacy clusters, and mass-retail anchors
> often perform modern market-center functions inside TURF fields: they gather
> repeated exchange, errands, comparison shopping, services, and regional
> identity into visible commercial terrain.

This module studies that function as an analogy. It does not claim that malls
or big-box fields are civic towns, historical descendants of market towns, or
official neighborhood boundaries.

## Research Questions

- Which TURF enclave families carry the strongest market-center signal?
- How do mall-led regional fields differ from edge-city big-box power fields?
- When does grocery/pharmacy density indicate everyday market-center terrain
  rather than a regional shopping anchor?
- Which fields look like declining or legacy market centers rather than active
  regional anchors?
- How should TURF describe these fields without making claims about consumer
  identity, culture, wealth, or neighborhood quality?
