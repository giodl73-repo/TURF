# A.2 Market Town To Mall

## Thesis

Malls, grocery/pharmacy clusters, retail complexes, and big-box fields can
function as modern market-center terrain inside TURF, especially where they
concentrate repeated everyday-service, regional-shopping, and anchor-store
signals.

This is a functional analogy. It is not a claim that malls descend directly
from medieval market towns, that retail complexes define culture, or that
commercial anchors are official civic boundaries.

  port/depot/corridor evidence.

  target boxes for ferry-side, dense-grid, highway-service, and Atlanta

  `tables/pizza-delivery-selected-node-summary-2026-08-16.csv` record the
