# Evidence Contract

## Allowed Evidence At Scaffold

A.3 may use only scoped TURF evidence packets and public context already cited
in the repo:

- enclave family consolidation,
- car-trip stack comparison,
- ferry context fixture,
- transit-center sidecar summaries,
- selected-node pizza sidecar,
- existing QSR, gas/convenience, and auto-parts sidecars,
- anchor-field target boxes.

The module may name source-gated evidence, but it must not treat source-gated
rows as observed absences.

## Required Layers

A.3 needs a movement-sidecar stack before making strong claims:

- ferry terminal or landing,
- transit center or station,
- airport-edge or passenger-transfer context,
- port, event, or industrial movement context,
- highway/interchange service,
- truck stop or travel center,
- motel/lodging where available,
- gas/convenience,
- QSR and pizza/carryout,
- auto-parts or repair/service.

The first scaffold can use existing gas/convenience, QSR, pizza, and auto-parts
evidence as partial proof. Ferry, depot/intermodal, truck-stop, lodging, and
logistics layers remain acquisition gates unless already observed in a scoped
packet.

## Claim Boundary

A.3 evidence supports movement-enclave terrain only. It does not support:

- freight-flow estimates,
- port authority boundaries,
- ferry catchments,
- commuter catchments,
- drive-time catchments,
- route choice,
- trip purpose,
- customer demand,
- service adequacy,
- official municipal or trade-area boundaries,
- direct historical continuity.
