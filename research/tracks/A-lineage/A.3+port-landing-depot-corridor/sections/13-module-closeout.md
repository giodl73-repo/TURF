# Module Closeout

## Scope

This closeout freezes A.3 as a reviewed movement-node module. It does not
claim every source gate is resolved. It says the module now has enough
reviewed evidence to serve the central spine without becoming a general
business inventory.

Output:

- `tables/a3-movement-node-closeout-summary-2026-08-16.csv`

## Closeout Read

A.3 supports four reusable movement roles:

- ferry-side landing / terminal terrain,
- depot / intermodal station-field terrain,
- highway / corridor service terrain,
- logistics / industrial facility-footprint terrain.

The module keeps Camp Creek as airport-edge retail / power-center terrain with
nearby cargo/Tradeport context, not as direct logistics packet evidence inside
the selected box.

## Reusable Rule

Ports, ferry landings, depots, airport edges, intermodal nodes, highway
corridors, and logistics facilities can function as modern movement-enclave
terrain inside TURF when they concentrate observed movement facilities and
supporting service layers.

The evidence is functional and bounded. It is not freight flow, commuter flow,
ferry catchment, drive-time catchment, official station area, port authority
boundary, route choice, trip purpose, demand, service adequacy, or direct
historical continuity.
