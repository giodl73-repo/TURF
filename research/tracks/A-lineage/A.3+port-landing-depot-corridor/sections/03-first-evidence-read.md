## Read

The first A.3 evidence split has four working movement types.

Ferry-side landing:

Kingston, Bainbridge Island, and Bremerton are already present in the Puget
Sound ferry context fixture. Kingston also now has a Domino's pizza/carryout
row in the A.2 selected-node pizza sidecar. That makes Kingston useful as a
thin ferry-side landing/provisioning example, not a service-absence case.

Port/event/industrial field:

South Philadelphia Sports / Port, Long Beach / Lakewood, Arlington / Grand
Prairie, and Midway / Ford City already appear in the enclave-family
consolidation as port/event/industrial/civic or related movement fields. These
are scaffold-ready examples, but they need port/event/logistics sidecars before
A.3 can make stronger movement claims.

Highway/corridor node:

Arlington / Grand Prairie is the strongest current proof point. The car-trip
stack identifies it as the only all-three dense grid across QSR, auto-parts,
and gas/convenience, and the A.2 pizza sidecar adds multi-brand pizza/carryout
presence. This is the best seed for the highway-node branch.

Depot/intermodal node:

Center City / Market East, University City / 30th Street, Loop / River North,
Camden Waterfront / Cooper, and Perimeter show rail, civic, and transit-adjacent
terrain in existing family or transit summaries. The depot analogy remains
less proven than the highway-node branch because station/intermodal evidence is
not yet standardized across the same target boxes.

## Decision

Proceed with A.3 as a scaffolded module. The first build should be a
movement-sidecar target set, not a claim packet:

1. Select seed boxes from ferry-side, port/event, highway-node, and
   depot/intermodal examples.
2. Reuse existing QSR, pizza, gas/convenience, and auto-parts evidence where it
   is already field-aligned.
3. Add or source-gate ferry terminal, transit center, truck-stop, motel,
   logistics, and port/event evidence.
4. Preserve source-gated rows adjacent to every summary.
