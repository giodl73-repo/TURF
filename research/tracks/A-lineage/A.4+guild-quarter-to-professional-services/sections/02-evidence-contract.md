# Evidence Contract

## Accepted Layers

A.4 may use:

- reviewed OSM bank / credit-union rows,
- reviewed Overture tax-prep exact-brand rows,
- reviewed Overture insurance exact-brand rows,
- future reviewed local professional-service rows if a stricter sidecar
  contract is added.

## Current Roles

- Finance fabric: bank and credit-union rows. This is a routine-service and
  civic-commercial fabric layer.
- Tax-prep selectivity: H&R Block, Jackson Hewitt, and Liberty Tax rows. This
  is a consumer paperwork / filing-service layer.
- Insurance breadth: State Farm, Allstate, Farmers, GEICO, Nationwide, and
  American Family rows. This is broad local agency/service fabric.
- Confirmed professional-services grid: a field where tax prep and insurance
  are both strong enough to indicate combined public-facing service depth.

## Exclusions

A.4 may not treat the following as packet-ready service-quarter evidence
without separate review:

- ATM-only rows,
- duplicate brand candidates,
- headquarters-only or back-office rows,
- online-only service pages,
- professional directories without a mapped public-facing facility,
- broad socioeconomic proxies.

## Claim Boundary

Rows are public service footprints. They do not establish customers, income,
credit, risk, assets, business quality, demand, catchment, trade area,
professional competence, or individual behavior.
