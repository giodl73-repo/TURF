# A.4 Guild Quarter / Professional Services

## Thesis

Banks, credit unions, insurance agencies, tax-prep offices, and local
professional-service storefronts can function as modern public-facing
service-quarter terrain inside TURF when they concentrate routine finance,
paperwork, advice, filing, and agency-service footprints.

This is a functional analogy. It is not a claim about guild continuity,
professional quality, income, credit, risk, wealth, customer identity,
protected traits, or individual behavior.
