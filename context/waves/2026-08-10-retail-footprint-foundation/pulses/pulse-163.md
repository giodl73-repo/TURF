## Result

car-oriented retail enclaves, combined corridors, food-forward daily-trip
fields, gas/highway grids with light auto-service, gas corridors with light
food, light support fields, light service nodes, and mixed stacks.

dimension. It also requires field-aligned QSR, auto-parts, and gas /
convenience evidence before a future field receives a stack type.
