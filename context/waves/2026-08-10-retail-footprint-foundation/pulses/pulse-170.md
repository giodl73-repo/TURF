## Claim

A.1 can now say that postal/address infrastructure is a recurring public
addressability layer in current TURF retail field evidence and a modern
parish-style analogy only at the level of function: local legibility, service
access, administrative routing, and civic presence.

## Boundary

The synthesis keeps four evidence classes separate:

- historical analogy,
- modern public infrastructure,
- observed commercial footprint,
- future model hypothesis.

It does not claim that USPS, ZIP Codes, ZCTAs, post offices, or TURF fields are
direct equivalents of parishes, market towns, or historical settlement
boundaries.
