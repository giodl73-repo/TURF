# Pulse 144: Sidecar Promotion Evidence Matrix

Date: 2026-08-13

## Intent

Decide what remains before scaling to another urban area by comparing completed
QSR and auto-parts sidecars against the current pre-scale field stacks.

## Changes

- Added a sidecar promotion evidence matrix query for Los Angeles / Inland
  Empire, Chicago, and Dallas/Fort Worth.
- Generated the 22-field promotion matrix CSV.
- Added the sidecar promotion evidence report.
- Linked the report from the README and recorded `.roles` review.

## Result

The matrix covers 22 field-aligned sidecar examples: seven Los Angeles /
Inland Empire fields, seven Chicago fields, and eight Dallas/Fort Worth fields.
Ten fields confirm service-corridor signal, six are QSR-forward and auto-light,
three are light on both sidecars, one is QSR-dense and auto-light, one adds
context without changing type, and one confirms a combined daily-trip and
trade/car-service grid.

The decision is to keep QSR and auto parts as standard sidecars before sixth
metro scale, but not promote them into the core cross-metro type-discovery
profile yet.

## Next

Create a standard sidecar readiness note before another metro is added. The
remaining choice is whether to retro-align Washington and Atlanta sidecars to
anchor fields or accept them as older metro/place-level evidence while the next
metro uses the five-layer core plus QSR and auto-parts sidecars.
