# Pulse 169: Postal-Parish Historical Analogy Gate

## Summary

Added the first historical analogy source notes to the A.1 postal-parish
module. The notes support a bounded functional analogy between older local
institutions and modern postal/addressability evidence without claiming direct
equivalence.

## Sources

- Historic England medieval settlement and market-town reports.
- UK Parliament / legislation.gov.uk local-government sources.
- National Archives poor-law, vestry, and parish-record examples.

## Boundary

The module may now use older local institutions as functional analogies for
local legibility, service-center access, administrative records, and settlement
identity. It still may not claim that USPS, ZIP Codes, ZCTAs, or TURF fields
are direct descendants or equivalents of those institutions.
