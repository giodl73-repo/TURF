# Pulse 174: Minimum Postal Parish Scaffold

## Summary

Scaffolded `A.1b+minimum-postal-parish` as an extension of A.1. The module
captures the hypothesis that the smallest modern TURF enclave may be a sparse
postal/addressability node: post office only, post office plus QSR, post office
plus gas/convenience, post office plus dollar store, or post office plus
grocery/pharmacy.
