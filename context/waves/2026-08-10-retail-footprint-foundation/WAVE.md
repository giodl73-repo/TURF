# Wave: Retail Footprint Foundation

## Goal

Create TURF as the public retail territory intelligence repo and prove the
first executable contract for turning chain store points into brand and city
footprint evidence.

## Thesis

The product is viable only if it separates public footprint inference from
unpublished corporate territory claims. This wave establishes the data-policy
boundary, a minimal POI intake shape, and a repeatable home-improvement fixture
before broader chain coverage begins.
