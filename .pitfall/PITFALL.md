## Integration

- ROLES: `.roles/ROLE.md` reviewers cover market geography, retail-operator
  usefulness, source policy, catchment modeling, atlas presentation, reusable
  kernel boundaries, and report contracts.
- VTRACE: TURF does not yet have root `docs/vtrace/`; PITFALL entries cite the
  README, data policy, wave/role review, reports, research modules, contracts,
  and Rust validation until VTRACE is introduced.
- Tests: Rust tests and CLI validators are evidence only when they preserve
  source-policy gates, schema/profile contracts, public-footprint wording, and
  source-gated claim boundaries.
